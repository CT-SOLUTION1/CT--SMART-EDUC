📌 Live Lesson Rules — Please Read Before Joining
  1. Use your correct and respectful name.
  2. Join only when you have permission and the correct lesson code.
  3. Respect the teacher and all other learners.
  4. Keep your microphone muted unless the teacher asks you to speak.
  5. Do not share the lesson code with unauthorised people.
  6. Do not record, screenshot, or share lesson content without permission.
  7. Follow the teacher's instructions throughout the lesson.
  8. The teacher may remove or reject anyone who breaks the rules.
